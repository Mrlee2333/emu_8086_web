## Memory notes

- Step-back history is the largest new consumer (bounded at 512 entries,
  typical classroom sessions use < 100 steps ≈ 6 MiB).
- Console buffer grows without bound: a print loop up to the 2 M
  instruction limit can accumulate millions of lines, growing memory and
  freezing the console render. **Known issue → fixed in v1.3.2**
  (2000-line cap).

## vs v1.3.0

- No change to the step core (`machine.step` untouched); throughput parity.
- Test suite +3 (step-back capture/restore).
